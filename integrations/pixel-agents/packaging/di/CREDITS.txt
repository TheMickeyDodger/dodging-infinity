CREDITS — Pixel Agents DI office package

Where these files are
  The dist/... and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt paths below name files of the BUILT package. In the
  source tree (integrations/pixel-agents/ in Dodging Infinity) the sources are:
    dist/assets/characters/char_0..5.png  <- webview-ui/public/assets/characters/char_0..5.png
    the font binary                       <- webview-ui/public/fonts/FSPixelSansUnicode-Regular.ttf
    its licence text                      <- packaging/di/OFL-1.1.txt
    dist/webview/fonts/InterVariable.woff2 <- webview-ui/public/fonts/InterVariable.woff2
    dist/webview/fonts/Inter-OFL.txt      <- webview-ui/public/fonts/Inter-OFL.txt
  scripts/build-di-package.mjs GENERATES dist/webview/fonts/OFL.txt (the font's embedded notices
  plus packaging/di/OFL-1.1.txt) and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt (from the installed packages' own
  licence files) when it assembles the package; neither is a source file.

Character art
  The office characters are based on the work of JIK-A-4: "MetroCity — Free Topdown
  Character Pack", https://jik-a-4.itch.io/metrocity-free-topdown-character-pack
  The source declares the pack CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). CC0 does not require
  attribution; this credit is retained deliberately, under the Mission's attribution
  instruction.
  The bundled character PNGs (dist/assets/characters/char_0..5.png) are derivatives that
  were generated in the Pixel Agents repository; their per-file correspondence to the
  source pack is declared by the project, not independently established.

Forest and observation-deck art
  The trees, undergrowth, rocks, deck railings and fittings, and the north gateway arch, braziers
  and planter (dist/assets/furniture/FOREST_*, DECK_* and GATEWAY_*) and the outdoor floor patterns (dist/assets/floors/floor_9..floor_13.png) are ORIGINAL
  pixel art drawn in code by this project's scripts/generate-forest-assets.mjs from a fixed seed
  — no external image, tileset or asset pack is used — and are covered by the MIT licence
  (LICENSE). No third-party licence obligation applies to them.

Metropolis world art
  Floor patterns floor_14..floor_27 (dist/assets/floors/) and the AUTUMN_* / HIGHLAND_* woodland
  variants (foliage recoloured from the FOREST_* art) are ORIGINAL, drawn in code by this
  project's scripts/generate-metropolis-floors.mjs and scripts/generate-biome-variants.mjs (MIT).
  LOUNGE_* are decor copies (bytes unchanged) of the bundled Pixel Agents seating art.
  The CITY_*, FARM_*, ANIMAL_*, DETAIL_*, COAST_*, AQUA_* and PET_* sprites were extracted by
  scripts/extract-atlas-sprites.mjs from seven source atlases generated for this project with
  OpenAI's built-in image generation; ASSET-PROVENANCE.json records, per sprite, its source atlas,
  that atlas's sha256 and the source rectangle. The source atlases themselves are not shipped.
