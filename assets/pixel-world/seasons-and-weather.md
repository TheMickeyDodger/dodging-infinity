# Seasons and regional weather: pictures and provenance

Five images of the **built** Pixel Agents DI office showing the City panel's seasons and regional weather. They were captured on 2026-10-23 from the **production package output** (`npm run build` then `npm run package`), served by the isolated fixture viewer, and show the **decorative** city only. They say nothing about Mission success, live agents or concurrency.

**What every picture is.** The living-metropolis world with a **synthetic FIXTURE office state** (the `crowded` fixture: 8 synthetic Missions, 48 synthetic live members), not a live provider. The season and the weather were chosen by clicking the City panel's own controls; the camera was moved with the existing e2e test hook `jumpToWorldPoint` (the `__PIXEL_AGENTS_E2E` flag, set by an init script, is the only seam). Each picture is a centre 800 x 500 clip of a 1600 x 1000 viewport at device pixel ratio 1, the real canvas pixels with nothing recoloured. Each caption bar was added over the picture as an HTML overlay (it covers part of the top of the clip); a 2 x 2 sheet is four such clips side by side. Every caption is the **state the page itself reported** (`getCityAtmosphere()`) immediately before the capture, never the state intended, and each says: built app, fixture office state (synthetic), and the clock. The files are standard 8-bit PNG with only IHDR, IDAT and IEND chunks (no text, EXIF or colour-profile data) and contain no URL or access token.

**What the pictures do and do not show.**
- Winter in the Autumn Wildwood reads as snow: measured on the built canvas, the winter ground is about hue 212 degrees, saturation 0.16 (it was hue 20, saturation 0.28, a rose-beige, before a warm winter district tint was made cool and neutral). Summer is unchanged.
- **River Valley Farmland keeps an olive-cream winter ground**, because its district tint is in the green band, which that rule leaves alone, so **a mild warm/cool seam remains where it meets the Highlands** (visible in the two-regions frame and in the Farmland sheet).
- Spring and summer look much alike where a district is mostly water, sand or paving (the Coast, the Harbour, much of the Metropolis); those districts are not pictured here.
- Light snow is small at 1:1 and faint on pale ground; heavy snow is larger flakes and a denser veil.
- Particles move with the world when you pan; a zoom re-seats the pattern. Indoor rooms and empty ground stay dry. Seasons and weather are decoration, never a forecast and never a sign of what an agent or a Mission is doing.
